AI Academy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

AI Academy is provided by the British School of Outdoor Education ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the AI Academy app and website collect, why, and your choices. We keep it short and specific.

Who can use AI Academy

AI Academy is for learners enrolled through our programmes, including the AI Academy for Teens (ages 13–18) and our professional AI courses. You sign in with the Microsoft account your programme gives you. We do not offer public sign-up.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Your name and account email (from your Microsoft sign-in)To show your profile, choose the courses available to you, and print your name on certificates.
Your account IDTo save your progress and certificates to your account so they sync between your phone and the web.
Lessons you completeTo show your progress and issue certificates when you finish a course.
Questions you ask the AI Tutor or AI NotesTo generate an answer. Your question is sent to our backend and to a Microsoft Azure AI model to produce the reply.
Certificates you earnSo that you, and anyone you share the certificate link with, can verify it.

Voice input

If you ask the AI Tutor a question by voice, your device's speech recognition (Apple on iPhone) turns it into text. We receive only the text, never the audio recording.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and we don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Microsoft Azure data centres in Sweden (EU). AI answers are generated by Microsoft Azure AI services, which do not use your questions to train their models.

Certificate verification

Each certificate has a unique ID. Anyone with that ID can see the learner name, course title, and issue date on our verification page, so they can confirm it is genuine. Only share your certificate link with people you want to see it.

How long we keep it

We keep your progress and certificates while your programme account is active, so you can continue learning and prove your achievements. When your account is removed by your programme, or if you ask us, we delete your progress and AI Academy data. We may keep certificate records so that previously issued certificates can still be verified, unless you ask us to remove them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing john@bsoedu.org. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian can also contact us on your behalf.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
