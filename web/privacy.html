AI Academy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

AI Academy is provided by B.S.O.E LTD (company number 08938266), trading as the British School of Outdoor Education ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the AI Academy app and website collect, why, and your choices. We keep it short and specific.

Who can use AI Academy

AI Academy is for learners aged 13 and over, including the AI Academy for Teens (ages 13–18) and our professional AI courses. You sign in with a Microsoft account in our AI Academy directory: your school, employer or programme may give you one, or you can create one in the app or when you subscribe on our website — we email you a link to set it up. Courses are available with the AI Academy All Access subscription or through an organisation that has arranged access for you (see our Terms of Use).

Voice input

If you ask the AI Tutor a question by voice, your device's speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) turns it into text. We receive only the text, never the audio recording.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and we don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts or payment card details, and we don't access your photos — only a file you choose to upload in the Study Companion.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Microsoft Azure data centres in Sweden (EU). AI answers are generated by Microsoft Azure AI services and, for the hub tools, by Anthropic. Neither uses your questions to train their models.

Certificate verification

Each certificate has a unique ID. Anyone with that ID can see the learner name, course title, and issue date on our verification page, so they can confirm it is genuine. Only share your certificate link with people you want to see it.

How long we keep it

We keep your progress and certificates while your account is active, so you can continue learning and prove your achievements. When you delete your account in the app (Menu → Account → Delete account), when your account is removed by your programme, or if you ask us, we delete your account, progress and AI Academy data. We keep subscription and transaction records only as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes. We may keep certificate records so that previously issued certificates can still be verified, unless you ask us to remove them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing john@bsoedu.org. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian can also contact us on your behalf.
