AI Academy

Delete your AI Academy account

AI Academy is provided by B.S.O.E LTD, trading as the British School of Outdoor Education. You can ask us to delete your AI Academy account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to delete your account

In the app

  1. Open AI Academy and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Email john@bsoedu.org from the address you use to sign in to AI Academy, with the subject "Delete my AI Academy account". We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

Accounts given to you by a school or programme

If your account was issued by a programme (for example an @teenskills.co.uk or @bsoedu.org address), the account itself is managed by that programme. Email us and we will delete your AI Academy data and ask the programme to remove the account.

What is deleted

  • Your AI Academy account (name, email address and account ID)
  • Your course progress and lesson activity
  • Your saved answers and reflections

Questions you ask the AI Tutor and the other AI tools are processed to give you an answer and are not kept in your account.

What we may keep

  • Subscription and transaction records — only as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Certificate records — we may keep these so that certificates already issued can still be verified. Ask us in your email if you want them removed too.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play — cancel it in your store account settings first.

See our Privacy Policy for full details.
